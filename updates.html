Week 4 update

Tue 24 Nov 2026

Done: Sieve now scans a repository and shows the real findings on the review screen, which was our early prototype goal for week 4.

  • Ali added Bandit, ESLint and Gitleaks next to Semgrep, each running in an isolated container, and built the findings store with its API, the scope check and the command-line interface. A scan of PyGoat gives 294 findings from four analyzers in about a minute and a half, and a scan of OWASP Juice Shop gives 1,681 findings in under two and a half minutes. He also wrote the rule-based grouping of duplicate findings and the matching of a repeated scan to the earlier one.
  • Riad finished the LLM provider interface that every AI step in Sieve uses: it works with six model providers, keeps each run within a budget, logs every call with its cost, and passes scanned code to the model only as data. In week 4 he started the false-positive check and wrote the part that finds the function around a finding in Python, Java, JavaScript and TypeScript code, which deduplication also uses to group findings.
  • Daniyal finished the evaluation harness and ran the baseline: the analyzers alone, before any AI check. On the OWASP Benchmark for Java they find 97% of the real vulnerabilities but also flag 53% of the cases that are not real, and on the Python Benchmark they find 53% and flag 15%. Those false alarms are what Sieve's false-positive check has to remove. He also started the report generator.
  • Furkan built the review screen: it lists the 153 Semgrep findings of a real PyGoat scan with filters for status, tool, severity and verdict, opens each finding with its code, and saves approve, reject and edit decisions through the API. A CVSS v4.0 score is always computed from its vector, never typed in.

Next: for the mid-demo on Fri 4 Dec, Ali adds the SpotBugs plug-in and scans WebGoat end to end, Riad adds the similarity and AI steps of deduplication and builds the false-positive check, Daniyal builds the report generator with Markdown export, and Furkan shows the review screen on triaged findings with the report preview.
